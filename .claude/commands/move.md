---
description: Move a machine to another site or route, send it for repair or take it out of service.
---

Run `npm run vending -- move <machine> [site] [--route=CODE] [--status=active|repair|removed] --actor=NAME`.

Before moving, show the machine's 28-day performance and the target site's commission and healthy food policy. Visits keep the site the machine stood at on the day, so past commission statements do not change.
